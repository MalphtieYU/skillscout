# Writing Task

**User:** Help me polish an English email.

## SkillScout Recommendation

### 1. My Read of Your Task

You want the wording of an email improved, based on text you provide.

**Confidence:** High Confidence

### 2. Tooling Decision

**Use Codex Native Only.** Editing text does not need mailbox access. Gmail is only useful when you want Codex to read a real thread, draft in the mailbox, or send a message.

### 3. Why This Decision

Native writing directly improves the result. Gmail would add privacy and send risk without adding value to a pasted draft.

### 4. Recommended Minimal Setup

| Tool / Plugin / Skill | Priority | Why it helps | Use it when | Skip it when | Plain-language explanation | 中文解释 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Codex Native Writing | Required | Rewrites tone and clarity | You provide the email text | You need mailbox actions | Improves the email without exposing inbox content | 直接润色文本，无需读取邮箱 |

### 5. Tools You Should Not Use for This Task

Do not use Gmail, Calendar, GitHub, Figma, or MCP. They add account access without improving a pasted email.

### 6. Better Workflow

1. Paste the draft and name the audience and desired tone.
2. Ask for a polished version and a shorter alternative.
3. Copy the preferred version into your email client yourself.

### 7. Copy-ready Codex Prompt

```text
Polish the English email below for a professional, warm tone. Keep the meaning unchanged, make it concise, and provide a shorter alternative. Do not access or send email.
```

### 8. Ask These First if Unclear

No extra questions needed.
